Inigo Quilez   ::     ::  

My name is Inigo Quilez, I grew up in San Sebastián / Donostia, a beautiful city in the Basque Country, northern Spain. I've worked in Computer Graphics as a Technical Artists, a Product Manager, a Software Engineer and a Research Engineer. I like publishing and sharing everything I discover or invent in this web site. If you find that useful, you can contribute to my Patreon or PayPal, but simply subscribing to my Youtube channel will also help.
